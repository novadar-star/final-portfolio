AI Jobs Scraper & Resume Optimizer 🔗

applying to jobs manually was slow, so i built a Slack-triggered pipeline that scrapes listings, scores them against my resume with an AI agent, and posts the results back to Slack. the interesting part wasn't the scraping. the AI kept sending back malformed JSON, so i wrote a parser: strip markdown fences, try JSON.parse, fall back to regex, apply safe defaults. that parser now lives in every AI workflow i build, and reusing it in a public webinar also meant sanitizing a hardcoded API key before sharing it. useful lesson.

automation · outbound · 2026

GTM Signal-Based Outbound Pipeline 🔗

ingests buying signals, scores leads, enriches contacts via Hunter.io, validates emails, and generates personalized outbound drafts. i originally planned to use NeverBounce for email validation, then found out it had usage costs at this volume, so i swapped it for a free MX record lookup plus a regex format check. catches the same invalid domains, costs nothing, and is easier to debug. also hit a JSearch nesting bug early on and fixed it with a normalization step before scoring. both workarounds ended up being better than the originals.
