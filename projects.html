AI jobs scraper and resume optimizer writeup

applying to jobs manually was slow, so i built a Slack-triggered pipeline that scrapes listings, scores them against my resume with an AI agent, and posts the results back. the interesting part was not the scraping. the model kept sending malformed JSON, so i wrote a parser: strip markdown fences, try JSON.parse, fall back to regex, apply safe defaults. that parser now lives in every AI workflow i build. reusing it in a public webinar also meant sanitizing a hardcoded API key before sharing it.

the parser became a reusable piece, and the workflow was demoed in a public webinar

Campus Catdex live

a shared record of Adamson's campus cats. students log where they saw a cat and add a photo. each one gets a guardian card they can share. there is no sign-in. so every write goes through SQL functions that check the input and limit how often each device can post. then someone uploaded a meme. i added trusted phones. a phone's first photo waits for my approval. after that it posts right away. everything runs on free tiers.

launched to the Adamson community, with zero paid services

vanilla JS · Neon Postgres · Vercel Functions · Vercel Blob

Squish Lab live

a squishy simulator where the sound is the point. every crackle, squelch and pop is made live from how hard and how fast you press. there are no sound files. eight squishies and six fillings, and some pairs have a character living in them to collect. you can squeeze with your hands on camera, and record a 10-second clip with the real sound for stories and reels. no framework and no build step.

every sound is made live in the browser, and hand tracking stays on the device

vanilla JS · Web Audio API · MediaPipe · Canvas

GTM signal outbound pipeline writeup

ingests buying signals, scores leads, enriches contacts via Hunter.io, validates emails, and drafts outbound. i planned to use NeverBounce, then found usage costs at this volume, so i swapped it for a free MX lookup plus a format check. same invalid domains, no extra bill. also hit a JSearch nesting bug and fixed it with a normalization step before scoring.
